Our approach

Remodeling that respects Pacific Northwest weather, and the people living through the work.

We are a Washington-registered general contractor focused on trade work: plumb posts, flat floors, watertight flashing, and a tidy handoff. We try to explain what we are doing and why, and to tell you early when conditions change.

How we work

From first conversation to final walkthrough

Four stages, so you know what comes next and what we need from you.

  1. 01

    Preconstruction

    We look at the space, talk through what you want, and go over budget and timing before any work is scheduled, so the scope is clear from the start.

  2. 02

    Permitting coordination

    Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we help prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections. We do not provide architectural or engineering services; if drawings or structural engineering are needed, independent licensed professionals prepare them.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Our crew carries out the agreed scope and keeps you posted on progress, schedule, and any change in conditions.

  4. 04

    Closeout

    A final walkthrough, a punch list for anything left to finish, and a handoff of paperwork and any inspection sign-offs.
