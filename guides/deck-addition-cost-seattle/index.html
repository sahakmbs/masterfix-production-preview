What usually moves the price

  • Material grade and lead times
  • Access, stories, and occupied-home constraints

FAQ

Common questions

What does Seattle MasterFix typically charge for deck addition cost Seattle?

Typical Seattle MasterFix project ranges, final price after free on-site estimate: starting about $6,480, typical about $17,100, high planning band about $27,000. Public Seattle market midpoint used for comparison is about $28,639. High end for elevated/complex sites; starting for smaller ground-level wood decks. Final price is after a free on-site estimate.

How much does a deck cost in Seattle?

Cost vs. Value 2025 Seattle lists about $20,294 for a wood deck addition scope and about $28,639 for composite, with estimated cost recouped around 83% and 80% respectively.

Do decks need permits in Seattle?

Many decks need SDCI permits; height, attachment, and location in required yards matter. See SDCI Tip #312 and confirm before building.

Does MasterFix build decks?

Yes. Seattle MasterFix provides full general contracting for deck additions in Seattle & King County, including permitting coordination under applicable SDCI / local rules.
