Service · Laminate

Laminate flooring

Laminate flooring installed to handle daily wear and damp weather

Laminate floors move with humidity, so the details matter. We let the planks acclimate, check the subfloor for flatness and moisture, lay the right underlayment, and leave expansion gaps at the edges.

Typical timeline

Typically 1–5 days for most homes, depending on room count and subfloor condition. Your written contract sets the actual schedule.

Materials

  • ·Laminate planks rated for the room's use
  • ·Underlayment for sound and moisture
  • ·Vapor barrier over concrete slabs

Homes built before 1978

Older homes may contain lead-based paint, and older materials such as siding, flooring, joint compound and texture may contain asbestos. If your home was built before 1978, tell us when you request an estimate. We will go over the lead-safety information required before work begins on a covered older home and discuss testing before disturbing suspect materials.

Permits

Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections, as set out in your written contract. Seattle MasterFix does not provide architectural or engineering services. If drawings or structural engineering are required, they are prepared by independent licensed professionals.

Avoid these

Common mistakes on this trade

Things worth watching for on any job, ours or someone else's.

01

Skipping acclimation

Planks need to adjust to the room's humidity before install; skipping it is a common cause of gaps or buckling.

02

No moisture barrier on a slab

Moisture coming up through concrete can damage flooring from below.

03

Tight edges

Floating floors need gaps at the walls so seasonal movement doesn't push the floor up.
