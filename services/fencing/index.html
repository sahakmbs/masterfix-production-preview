Service · Fencing

Fencing

Wood fences and gates built to stand through wet winters

A fence has to stay straight through wind and soggy soil, and look good doing it. We set posts in concrete, choose lumber suited to ground contact versus panels, and hang gates on hardware matched to their size and weight.

Typical timeline

Typically 3–10 days for residential runs, depending on length, terrain and weather. Your written contract sets the actual schedule.

Materials

  • ·Cedar and pressure-treated lumber
  • ·Concrete footings
  • ·Gate hinges and latches sized to the gate

Property lines and utilities

You are responsible for confirming where your property line is; a survey may help if it is unclear. Before digging, utility locates are requested through Washington’s “811” call-before-you-dig service.

Permits

Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections, as set out in your written contract. Seattle MasterFix does not provide architectural or engineering services. If drawings or structural engineering are required, they are prepared by independent licensed professionals.

Avoid these

Common mistakes on this trade

Things worth watching for on any job, ours or someone else's.

01

Guessing at property lines

Confirm your boundary before posts go in. A survey may be worth it if the line is unclear; boundary disputes with neighbors are expensive.

02

Shallow posts

A fence is only as strong as what's below grade. Depth and drainage help keep it from leaning.

03

Underbuilt gates

Sagging gates are a common complaint. Hinges and bracing should match the gate's weight.
