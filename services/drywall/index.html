Service · Drywall

Drywall

Hanging, taping, and finishing drywall ready for paint

Good drywall work disappears once the room is painted. We hang board with the right fastener pattern, build the joints up over several coats, match texture on repairs where we can, and contain dust so the rest of your home stays clean.

Typical timeline

Typically 3–10 days including drying time between coats. Your written contract sets the actual schedule.

Materials

  • ·Standard, moisture-resistant and fire-rated gypsum board
  • ·Joint compound suited to the coat schedule
  • ·Corner bead and texture materials

Homes built before 1978

Older homes may contain lead-based paint, and older materials such as siding, flooring, joint compound and texture may contain asbestos. If your home was built before 1978, tell us when you request an estimate. We will go over the lead-safety information required before work begins on a covered older home and discuss testing before disturbing suspect materials.

Permits

Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections, as set out in your written contract. Seattle MasterFix does not provide architectural or engineering services. If drawings or structural engineering are required, they are prepared by independent licensed professionals.

Cost drivers

What typically moves the price. We talk through these early, before work starts.

  • Square footage to hang versus finish only
  • Level of finish (smooth and paint-ready, or textured)
  • Fire-rated or moisture-resistant board requirements
  • Matching texture on existing walls
  • Dust containment and whether the space is occupied

Avoid these

Common mistakes on this trade

Things worth watching for on any job, ours or someone else's.

01

Rushing coats

Joints are typically built up over several coats with drying time between each. Rushing shows up as seams under paint.

02

Ignoring raking light

Light from windows and fixtures reveals flaws. Checking the finish under the room's real lighting helps.

03

Wrong board type

Garages, baths and shared walls often call for fire- or moisture-rated board under the building code.
