Tile

Tile for bathrooms, kitchens, and floors

Every seam and grout joint stays visible for as long as the tile does. We flatten the substrate, plan the layout before setting anything, and put waterproofing behind tile in wet areas.

Homes built before 1978

Older homes may contain lead-based paint, and older materials such as siding, flooring, joint compound and texture may contain asbestos. If your home was built before 1978, tell us when you request an estimate. We will go over the lead-safety information required before work begins on a covered older home and discuss testing before disturbing suspect materials.

Permits

Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections, as set out in your written contract. Seattle MasterFix does not provide architectural or engineering services. If drawings or structural engineering are required, they are prepared by independent licensed professionals.

Avoid these

Common mistakes on this trade

Things worth watching for on any job, ours or someone else's.

01

Skipping waterproofing

In a shower the waterproofing behind the tile keeps leaks out, not the grout on the surface.

02

Ignoring flatness

Uneven tile edges (lippage) usually trace back to an uneven surface underneath. Leveling comes first.

03

Rushing large-format tile

Large tile needs proper bedding and cure time; rushing can cause cracks months later.
