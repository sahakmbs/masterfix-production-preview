Housing in this area

What the houses in Kent are often like

This is general context for the neighborhood or city, not a claim about a specific property we have already worked on.

Kent includes mid-century and later houses on the East Hill, older streets near downtown, and a wide mix of later subdivisions. The valley and the hill have different access and, in some cases, different drainage conditions. Many houses from the 1970s through the 1990s still have their original baths and siding. Some neighborhoods have HOA or plat rules. We quote from the house and the site, not from a single city-wide figure.

Permits

Permit notes for Kent

Educational only — not legal advice. Rules change. Verify with the jurisdiction before work starts.

Kent is not under Seattle SDCI, and it is not on the same permit portal as several Eastside cities. The City of Kent Economic and Community Development department issues permits through its own system. Do not assume MyBuildingPermit.com or Seattle tip sheets apply. Confirm current building, plumbing, and electrical requirements with the City of Kent for your address. We identify the jurisdiction on the estimate and include likely permit needs in the written quote when they apply.

How we typically work

From first call to finished work

The same four steps on every job, in every area we serve.

  1. 01

    Preconstruction

    We look at the space, talk through what you want, and go over budget and timing before any work is scheduled, so the scope is clear from the start.

  2. 02

    Permitting coordination

    Where a permit is required for the work we perform, we help prepare and submit the application and coordinate inspections. We do not provide architectural or engineering services; if drawings or structural engineering are needed, independent licensed professionals prepare them.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Our crew carries out the agreed scope and keeps you posted on progress, schedule, and any change in conditions.

  4. 04

    Closeout

    A final walkthrough, a punch list for anything left to finish, and a handoff of paperwork and any inspection sign-offs.
